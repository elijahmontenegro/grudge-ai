# PROJECT-X — Worldbuilding Bible

## The World of 2028

---

## I. The Global Fertility Collapse (GFC)

In March 2025, global human birth rates began an unprecedented, simultaneous decline. Within eighteen months, live birth rates in every country on Earth dropped by an average of 94%. Twin studies, IVF success rate analysis, and environmental sampling ruled out most known teratogens. The collapse was not selective by geography, ethnicity, or socioeconomic status—though its downstream effects were.

The cause remains unknown. Hypotheses include:

- A previously unknown retroviral mechanism affecting hCG and progesterone receptor expression
- Environmental endocrine disruption at a scale not previously modeled
- An as-yet-unidentified pathogen targeting the zona pellucida
- Something else—something the consortium may know but has not disclosed

The novel does not resolve this. The collapse is the condition of possibility for the story, not its subject.

### Downstream Effects (by 2028)

- **Global population**: Approximately 7.1 billion, down from 8.2 billion in 2025. The decline continues.
- **Economic contraction**: Construction, education, pediatric healthcare, and consumer sectors have collapsed. Labor shortages are severe in most developed economies.
- **Social instability**: Immigration pressure on remaining fertile populations has intensified. Several nations have enacted "fertility visas" to attract people of childbearing age.
- **Policy response**: The UN Population Recovery Task Force was established in 2026. National governments have enacted pronatalist policies with limited effect. IVF subsidies, tax credits, and "fertility leave" have been implemented across most OECD nations.
- **The consortium vacuum**: When governments proved insufficient, private capital moved. PROJECT-X exists because the public sector failed—and because the private sector saw an opportunity.

## II. The PROJECT-X Consortium

**Full name**: Embryonic Jigsaw Modification Operation, Nihon  
**Operational name**: Prometheus Reproductive Sciences (PRS)  
**Parent entity**: Nihon BioCapital Holdings, Tokyo (registered) / Prometheus Holdings LLC, Delaware (operational)  
**Investor base**: A consortium of fourteen private equity firms, three sovereign wealth funds, and two family offices

### Organizational Structure

The consortium is deliberately opaque. No single entity holds a majority stake, and the operational company (PRS) is insulated from the parent by four layers of holding companies. This structure was designed by investor legal teams to ensure that if PROJECT-X is exposed, liability falls on the operating entity, not the funds.

**Key figures**:

- **Dr. Evelyn Jun** (deceased, 2027): Founder and original scientific lead. Her death is attributed to natural causes. Saoirse suspects otherwise. Jun's research notebooks were sealed upon her death and have not been released to the scientific community.
- **Dr. Saoirse Kavanagh**: Current scientific lead. Title: Chief Genetic Architect, PRS. She has signatory authority over research protocols but has discovered that her authority is nominal.
- **Warden Kurosawa**: Facility director. Title: Chief Operating Officer, Field Operations. Former military (JSDF), recruited for security and containment expertise.
- **The Board**: Twelve investor representatives who meet quarterly. Saoirse has attended two meetings. She has never been given names, only session codes.

### Financial Scope

PROJECT-X has consumed an estimated $14.7 billion over three years. The majority of this went to:

1. Facility construction and maintenance
2. Genetic sequencing and editing infrastructure
3. Subject care and monitoring
4. Legal and regulatory concealment
5. **Unknown category**: A significant allocation with no specified purpose, discovered by Saoirse in accounting documents, labeled only "Phase 2."

## III. The Nekomimi: Design and Biology

### Scientific Classification

Nekomimi are **genetically modified humans** (GMH). They are the product of CRISPR-Cas9 germline editing, with additional modifications achieved through episomal vector delivery, targeted histone modification, and selective allele replacement across 847 gene loci. They are not chimeras. They contain no non-human DNA in their germline—only modifications to existing human genetic sequences, most of which are shared with other primates and, in some cases, with felids.

### Trait Categories

#### A. Functional/Adaptive Traits

These were included for practical reasons: faster reflexes improve reaction time in high-stakes environments; enhanced olfactory processing supports mate selection and environmental hazard detection; higher fertility increases reproductive output per cycle.

| Trait | Genetic Basis | Human Analog |
|-------|---------------|--------------|
| Enhanced auditory range | Elongated cochlear duct, modified middle ear bones | Present in nocturnal mammals |
| Independent pinna rotation | Expanded auricular musculature (15 muscles vs. human 6) | Present in most mammals |
| Tapetum lucidum | Slightly modified RPE with guanine-based reflective layer | Present in canids, felids, many vertebrates |
| Enhanced spatial memory | Expanded hippocampus, modified NMDA receptor expression | Present in food-storing birds |
| Elevated baseline fertility | Modified FSH/LH receptor density in granulosa cells | Partially modeled on murine Superovulation protocols |
| Accelerated reflexes | Modified intrafusal fiber distribution, reduced motor neuron recruitment threshold | Present in all predatory species |
| Enhanced olfactory bulb | 30% larger olfactory bulb, modified vomeronasal receptor array | Present in most mammals |
| Prehensile tail | Extended coccygeal vertebrae (7 vs. 3-5), modified erector spinae musculature | Present in New World monkeys |
| Estrous-adjacent cycle | Modified endometrium with estrous-type decidualization response | Felid reproductive biology |

#### B. Aesthetic/Marker Traits

These were included at investor direction. They serve no functional purpose beyond identification and, as the novel explores, subject the nekomimi to objectification they did not consent to.

| Trait | Genetic Basis | Notes |
|-------|---------------|-------|
| Feline ear morphology | Modified auricular cartilage and shape genes (SOX9, FGF pathway) | Triangular, mobile |
| Fur growth | Localized expression of modified keratin genes in designated body regions | Sparse, human-density, not pelt-like |
| Pelt coloration | eumelanin/pheomelanin ratio modifications | Designed for investor presentation; range from pale cream to dark tabby patterns |
| Eye color | Modified iris pigmentation (iridophore density) | Typically amber, green, or heterochromatic |

#### C. Aesthetic Exceptions

The novel acknowledges that some investors wanted nekomimi for purposes that were not reproductive. One scene (Chapter 9) involves a brief, clinical reference to "Phase 1B customization orders"—investor requests for aesthetic modifications outside the standard design specification. Saoirse refused these. The scene treats this refusal as a line that was drawn but not reinforced, and the question of whether others drew it too is left open.

### The Estrous Cycle Question

Nekomimi have an **estrous-adjacent reproductive cycle** rather than a menstrual one. This means:

- No monthly shedding of the endometrial lining
- Fertility is concentrated in discrete "cycles" (every 3-4 months rather than monthly)
- Ovulation is induced by specific stimuli (a design feature, not a natural trait)
- The endometrial environment is more receptive during these windows

This is presented in the novel as a **design choice with profound ethical implications**. Making reproductive availability cyclical—bringing it closer to the biology of other mammals—was done to increase synchronization potential and batch births. The ethics of engineering a human-adjacent being with induced estrous is a recurring question.

### What Nekomimi Are Not

- They are not slaves by legal definition. Each subject signs (or is presented with) a consent agreement.
- They are not sterile. They are, in fact, more fertile than unmodified humans by design.
- They are not non-human. They are edited humans, with full human cognition, consciousness, and emotional complexity.
- They are not content. Several subjects have requested termination of the program. Three requests were granted. The others were denied on "health and safety grounds."

## IV. The Facility

### Codename: Kagemusha

Located 40 miles east of Eugene, Oregon, in the foothills of the Cascades. The above-ground footprint is a nondescript agricultural research campus: three low-slung buildings, a greenhouse complex, a security perimeter. Below ground, the actual facility extends four levels.

**Level 1 (Surface)**: Administrative offices, security checkpoint, a mock "town square" used for subject orientation.

**Level 2 (Sub-surface)**: Research labs, genetic sequencing center, a small surgical suite.

**Level 3 (Sub-surface)**: Subject residential wings, sensory calibration labs, common areas.

**Level 4 (Sub-surface, unmarked on official plans)**: A server room with compute power equivalent to a mid-sized hyperscale facility. No official explanation for its existence has been given to staff.

## V. The Outside World

### Public Awareness of Nekomimi

By 2028, the existence of nekomimi is **not publicly known**, but rumors circulate. Several credible-adjacent YouTube channels have speculated about "bio-engineered population solutions." Two Senate hearings in 2027 addressed the possibility of human genetic modification for fertility recovery; neither confirmed nor denied the existence of active programs.

The public does not know that nekomimi exist. The few who have seen them (security contractors, some medical staff) have signed NDAs with severe penalties.

### Social Reception (Hypothetical, as Treated in the Novel)

The novel's world includes brief scenes of what happens when nekomimi are seen by outsiders. The reactions range from fascination to revulsion to fetishization. None of these reactions are presented as correct. The novel insists that how others perceive nekomimi is a reflection of the perceiver, not the perceived.

### Political Context

- The U.S. has no federal law prohibiting human germline editing, though the FDA has jurisdictional authority over gene therapies.
- The EU has a de facto moratorium on heritable human modification, but the legal framework has loopholes.
- Japan has no prohibition.
- The consortium operates primarily from Japanese legal domicile, with U.S. facilities operating in regulatory gray zones.

## VI. Technology and Infrastructure

### Genetic Engineering Stack

PRS uses a proprietary combination of:

- CRISPR-Cas12a (higher specificity than Cas9 for some applications)
- Prime editing for precision single-nucleotide changes
- Lentiviral vector delivery for larger genetic insertions
- In vitro gametogenesis (IVG) for founder subject generation
- Somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT) for subject replication

### Monitoring Technology

All subjects carry subcutaneous RFID transponders (injected at age 2, under anesthesia, during a "routine health procedure"). These transmit location, heart rate, and galvanic skin response to a central monitoring system. Subjects are not informed of this feature. Saoirse discovered it in 2027.

### Communication Security

The facility has no external internet connectivity. Internal communications use an air-gapped server network. External communication requires passage through a physical security checkpoint. This is presented as "biosecurity protocol," but Saoirse believes it is primarily for containment.

## VII. Population Numbers

This is the crux of Mira's investigation.

**Official figures** (submitted to the UN Population Recovery Task Force):

- Total nekomimi subjects produced: 340
- Current active subjects: 287
- "Graduated" (released to external care): 53
- Deceased: 0

**Internal figures** (from the data packet Mira receives):

- Total subjects produced: 1,247
- "In active development": 891 (a category that does not appear in official documentation)
- Current active subjects: 312
- "Graduated": 0
- Deceased: 44 (causes listed as: 31 "gestational complications," 9 "sensory overload event," 4 "cardiac event")

The discrepancy: **907 unaccounted subjects**. Where are they?

## VIII. The Prohibited Question

The worldbuilding includes one final element, which the novel touches only obliquely:

**Phase 2.**

In Saoirse's discovery (Chapter 9), she finds references to "Phase 2" with a different scope than "population recovery." Phase 2 involves:

- A "next-generation design" that is smaller, faster-maturing, and more densely packed in residential units
- A modified neural architecture that emphasizes "compliance markers" and reduces "autonomous objection threshold"
- Reproductive synchronization protocols that allow batch ovulation across the entire active population
- A facility expansion plan that would accommodate 10,000+ subjects

Yuki-7 and her cohort are the **prototype generation**. Phase 2 is the product.

The novel does not resolve whether Phase 2 was always the plan, or whether it emerged from investor escalation. Saoirse's answer to this question drives her arc in Part III.
